About PII Guardrails

The PII guardrail uses Microsoft Presidio to detect and redact sensitive information before it reaches the LLM. When enabled for a team, it scans for: email addresses, phone numbers, credit card numbers, SSNs, IP addresses, and person names. Detected PII is replaced with placeholders like [EMAIL_ADDRESS] or [PERSON].

Note: This demonstrates the pattern for privacy protection. It does not guarantee compliance with any specific regulation (HIPAA, GDPR, etc.).
